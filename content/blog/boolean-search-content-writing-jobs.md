Content writing job listings are a mess of overlapping titles. The same role might be posted as "content writer," "copywriter," "content strategist," "technical writer," "UX writer," "blog writer," "editorial assistant," or "content marketing specialist." Some are pure creative writing, others skew toward SEO, social media, documentation, or B2B marketing.

Boolean search helps you zero in on the specific kind of writing work you want. By combining title variations, adding industry or format keywords, and excluding unrelated gigs (like grant writing or journalism), you build searches that surface the right roles faster. ## Why Content Writing Searches Are So Noisy

Job boards lump writing roles into a single "writing" or "content" bucket. A search for "writer" returns:

- Creative copywriter positions at ad agencies
- Technical documentation roles at SaaS companies
- Grant writing jobs at nonprofits
- Journalism and editorial positions
- Ghostwriting and book-writing gigs
- Social media caption writing
- SEO blog writing for affiliate sites

Without Boolean operators, you scroll through dozens of irrelevant listings before finding one that matches your niche.

## Boolean String Templates for Content Writing Jobs

### General Content Writer / Copywriter

```
("content writer" OR copywriter OR "content strategist") (b2b OR SaaS OR blog OR "long-form") -grant -journalist -editorial -"social media"
```

This targets mid-to-long-form content writing while cutting out journalism, grant writing, and pure social media roles.

### Technical Writer

```
("technical writer" OR "documentation specialist" OR "API writer") (SaaS OR software OR "developer documentation" OR "user guides") -grant -copywriter
```

Focuses on software/developer-facing technical writing and drops copywriting or grant roles.

### UX Writer / Content Designer

```
("UX writer" OR "content designer" OR "product writer") (microcopy OR "in-product" OR onboarding OR "error messages")
```

Surfaces UX writing roles that sit inside product teams, distinct from marketing content.

### SEO Blog Writer

```
("blog writer" OR "SEO writer" OR "content writer") (SEO OR "search engine" OR "keyword research" OR "traffic") -technical -copywriter
```

Pulls SEO-focused content roles and avoids technical writing and pure copywriting.

### B2B SaaS Content Marketing

```
("content marketing" OR "content writer") (SaaS OR B2B OR "lead generation" OR "case studies" OR "white papers") -"social media manager"
```

Targets B2B content marketing roles that produce bottom-of-funnel assets like case studies and white papers.

### Entry-Level / Junior Content Jobs

```
("junior" OR "entry-level" OR "associate") ("content writer" OR copywriter OR "editorial assistant") -senior -lead -"5+ years"
```

Finds early-career writing jobs and excludes senior-level postings.

## Mixing in Industry Keywords

Add industry terms to narrow by domain:

- **Finance**: `fintech OR banking OR "personal finance"`
- **Healthcare**: `healthcare OR "health tech" OR medical`
- **Ecommerce**: `ecommerce OR "direct-to-consumer" OR DTC`
- **Education**: `edtech OR "online learning" OR curriculum`
- **Developer tools**: `DevTools OR API OR "developer experience"`

Example: combine with the general content writer template:

```
("content writer" OR copywriter) (fintech OR banking OR "personal finance") -grant -journalist
```

## Location-Based Content Writing Searches

Add location or remote filters to any template:

```
("content writer" OR copywriter) (remote OR "work from home") (SaaS OR B2B)
```

For hybrid or on-site, replace `remote` with the city:

```
("content writer" OR copywriter) ("New York" OR NYC OR Brooklyn) (SaaS OR B2B)
```

## Searching Specific Job Boards

### LinkedIn Jobs

Use the same strings in LinkedIn's keyword search. LinkedIn supports Boolean operators natively:

```
("content writer" OR copywriter) (SaaS OR B2B) NOT grant NOT journalist NOT editorial
```

### Indeed

Indeed supports Boolean with `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, and parentheses:

```
("content writer" OR copywriter) AND (SaaS OR B2B) NOT (grant OR journalist)
```

### Google for Jobs

Use `site:` to search specific career pages:

```
site:greenhouse.io OR site:lever.co ("content writer" OR copywriter) (SaaS OR remote)
```

## Pro Tips

- **Title inflation is real** — "content strategist" at one company is "content writer" at another. Always search both.
- **Portfolio keywords matter more than titles** — add `("writing samples" OR portfolio)` to find listings that ask for one (these are typically higher-quality roles).
- **Exclude industries you dislike** — add `-gambling -crypto -"adult content"` if those aren't your thing.
- **Save your best searches as job alerts** — most job boards let you turn a Boolean query into an email alert.
