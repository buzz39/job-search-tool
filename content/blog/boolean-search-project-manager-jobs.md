Project manager searches get noisy fast. A simple search for "project manager" can pull in construction roles, IT delivery jobs, marketing project coordinator postings, implementation manager openings, scrum master jobs, and senior program management roles all at once. Some may be perfect; many will be completely wrong for your background.

Boolean search helps you separate the project work you actually want from adjacent titles and industries that crowd the results. By grouping title variations, adding methodology or domain keywords, and excluding recurring false positives, you can make LinkedIn, Indeed, Google, and company career pages return much cleaner job matches. If you want a fast starting point, the [Job Search Query Builder](/) can turn your preferred titles, skills, and exclusions into a reusable search string.

## Why Project Manager Searches Are Hard

Project management is a function, not a single job category. Employers use the same title for very different responsibilities, including:

- software delivery and technical project management
- implementation and customer onboarding projects
- marketing campaigns and creative operations
- construction, facilities, and real estate projects
- operations improvement and process initiatives
- agile scrum master or delivery manager roles
- enterprise program management and PMO jobs

That variety is useful if you are open to several paths, but frustrating when you need a targeted pipeline. Boolean operators let you define both sides of the search: the titles and keywords you want to include, plus the obvious mismatches you want to remove.

## Core Boolean Operators for Project Manager Searches

Use these building blocks before adapting the longer examples below.

**Quotes** keep exact job titles together:

```text
"project manager"
```

**OR** captures title variations:

```text
("project manager" OR "project coordinator" OR "delivery manager")
```

**AND** adds required context:

```text
("project manager" OR "delivery manager") AND SaaS
```

**NOT** removes unrelated roles:

```text
("project manager" OR "program manager") AND software NOT construction NOT facilities
```

On Google-style searches, use the minus sign for exclusions:

```text
("project manager" OR "delivery manager") software -construction -facilities -estimator
```

## Boolean Strings by Project Management Track

### Software and IT Project Manager Roles

If you want technology project management, include both title variations and technical delivery language:

```text
("IT project manager" OR "technical project manager" OR "software project manager" OR "delivery manager") AND (software OR SaaS OR cloud OR implementation) NOT construction NOT facilities
```

For roles that require agile experience, add methodology keywords:

```text
("technical project manager" OR "delivery manager" OR "scrum master") AND (agile OR scrum OR Jira OR sprint) AND (software OR engineering) NOT construction
```

If you are targeting remote-friendly tech companies, combine remote terms with software context:

```text
("project manager" OR "technical project manager" OR "delivery manager") AND (remote OR "work from home" OR distributed) AND (SaaS OR software) NOT construction NOT healthcare
```

### Implementation and Customer Onboarding Roles

Implementation project management roles are often posted under implementation manager, onboarding manager, solutions delivery, or customer success operations titles:

```text
("implementation project manager" OR "implementation manager" OR "customer onboarding manager" OR "solutions delivery manager") AND (SaaS OR software OR platform) NOT sales NOT "account executive"
```

For more technical implementation roles, require integration or systems language:

```text
("implementation manager" OR "technical project manager" OR "solutions delivery") AND (API OR integrations OR configuration OR migration) AND SaaS NOT developer
```

For early-career implementation roles, add coordinator and specialist titles:

```text
("implementation coordinator" OR "onboarding specialist" OR "project coordinator") AND (SaaS OR software OR client) NOT sales NOT recruiter
```

### Marketing and Creative Project Manager Roles

Marketing project management searches can get mixed with product marketing, account management, and agency sales roles. Use campaign and workflow keywords to keep results relevant:

```text
("marketing project manager" OR "creative project manager" OR "campaign project manager") AND (campaigns OR creative OR content OR brand) NOT "account executive" NOT sales
```

For agency or creative operations roles:

```text
("creative project manager" OR "producer" OR "agency project manager") AND (traffic OR resourcing OR timelines OR deliverables) NOT video NOT film
```

For digital marketing delivery roles, add channel language:

```text
("digital project manager" OR "marketing project manager") AND (web OR email OR paid media OR SEO) NOT developer NOT designer
```

### Construction and Facilities Project Manager Roles

If you do want construction or facilities project management, include those terms directly instead of relying on the generic title:

```text
("construction project manager" OR "facilities project manager" OR "real estate project manager") AND (budget OR vendor OR schedule OR site) NOT software NOT SaaS
```

For assistant or coordinator-level construction roles:

```text
("assistant project manager" OR "project coordinator") AND (construction OR general contractor OR permitting) NOT software
```

For owner-side facilities roles:

```text
("facilities project manager" OR "workplace project manager") AND (renovation OR vendor OR capital projects) NOT residential
```

### PMO and Program Management Roles

Program management and PMO searches need careful wording because "program manager" can mean anything from nonprofit operations to technical strategy. Start with governance and portfolio terms:

```text
("PMO manager" OR "program manager" OR "portfolio manager") AND (governance OR roadmap OR portfolio OR stakeholders) NOT nonprofit NOT education
```

For enterprise technology program management:

```text
("technical program manager" OR "IT program manager" OR "enterprise program manager") AND (cloud OR migration OR security OR platform) NOT product NOT construction
```

For senior project leadership roles:

```text
("senior project manager" OR "program manager" OR "project director") AND (stakeholders OR budget OR roadmap OR cross-functional) NOT construction NOT facilities
```

## Platform-Specific Tips

### LinkedIn

LinkedIn often performs best with compact strings. Start with a title group and one or two required keywords, then use LinkedIn filters for location, remote status, experience level, and date posted.

```text
("technical project manager" OR "delivery manager") AND SaaS
```

If results are too broad, add exclusions one at a time. Very long negative lists can make LinkedIn behave unpredictably.

### Indeed

Indeed usually rewards simpler wording. Try exact title phrases with a few core skills rather than a giant Boolean expression:

```text
"project manager" Jira agile remote
```

Run separate searches for different tracks, such as software project manager, construction project manager, and marketing project manager, so you can tune each one independently.

### Google

Google is useful for searching company career pages and applicant tracking systems directly:

```text
("technical project manager" OR "delivery manager") (remote OR "United States") (SaaS OR software) -construction
```

To search common ATS pages, use focused X-ray searches:

```text
site:greenhouse.io "technical project manager" remote SaaS -construction
```

```text
site:lever.co "implementation project manager" SaaS remote
```

```text
site:ashbyhq.com "delivery manager" software remote
```

## Exclusions That Save Time

The right exclusions depend on your target track. For software or SaaS project manager searches, common false positives include:

```text
NOT construction NOT facilities NOT estimator NOT superintendent NOT "project engineer" NOT residential
```

For marketing project management, you may want to remove sales-heavy postings:

```text
NOT "account executive" NOT sales NOT recruiter NOT internship
```

For construction project management, do the opposite and remove tech-only postings:

```text
NOT software NOT SaaS NOT agile NOT scrum
```

Avoid copying every exclusion into every search. Start with the false positives you actually see, then add negative terms only when they improve the results.

## A Simple Workflow for Better Project Manager Searches

1. Pick your lane: software, implementation, marketing, construction, facilities, or PMO.
2. Write three to six title variations for that lane.
3. Add two context keywords such as SaaS, Jira, campaigns, construction, integrations, or governance.
4. Run the search and scan the first page for repeated irrelevant results.
5. Add only the exclusions that remove real noise.
6. Save the best query and reuse it for job alerts.

## Bottom Line

Project manager job titles are broad, but your search does not have to be. Boolean search lets you translate your preferred industry, methodology, and seniority into a precise query that filters out the wrong kind of project work. Build a few role-specific strings, test them on multiple platforms, and keep refining the exclusions as you learn which postings waste your time.
