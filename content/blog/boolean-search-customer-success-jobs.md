Customer success job searches can get noisy fast. The same post-sales function might be advertised as "customer success manager," "client success manager," "account manager," "implementation specialist," "customer onboarding manager," or "customer experience consultant." Some roles are strategic and revenue-facing, while others are closer to technical support, renewals, training, or project delivery.

Boolean search helps you separate the roles you actually want from broad customer service results. By grouping title variations, adding product or industry keywords, and excluding unrelated support or sales postings, you can build searches that return stronger matches in less time. If you want a shortcut, the [Job Search Query Builder](/) can turn your preferred titles, skills, and location into a reusable search string.

## Why Customer Success Searches Need Better Keywords

Customer success sits between support, sales, product, and operations, so job boards often mix together very different roles. A search for "customer success" may include:

- customer support representative jobs
- call center and help desk roles
- account executive positions mislabeled as customer success
- renewals or collections jobs
- implementation roles that require heavy technical experience
- senior leadership jobs when you want an individual contributor role

Boolean operators let you describe what should appear in the posting and what should be filtered out.

## Core Boolean Operators for Customer Success Searches

Use these building blocks before adapting the examples below.

**Quotes** keep exact phrases together:

```text
"customer success manager"
```

**OR** captures title variations:

```text
("customer success manager" OR "client success manager" OR CSM)
```

**AND** adds required context:

```text
("customer success manager" OR CSM) AND SaaS
```

**NOT** removes unrelated postings:

```text
("customer success manager" OR CSM) AND SaaS NOT "call center" NOT "account executive"
```

On Google-style searches, a minus sign may work better than `NOT`:

```text
("customer success manager" OR CSM) SaaS -"call center" -"account executive"
```

## Boolean Strings by Customer Success Role

### Customer Success Manager Roles

Use this string when you want classic CSM roles focused on adoption, retention, and customer outcomes:

```text
("customer success manager" OR "client success manager" OR CSM OR "customer success specialist") AND (retention OR adoption OR onboarding OR "business reviews") NOT "call center" NOT "customer service representative"
```

For SaaS customer success roles, add product and account-management language:

```text
("customer success manager" OR CSM OR "client success manager") AND (SaaS OR software OR platform) AND (renewals OR adoption OR "QBR" OR "customer health") NOT support NOT "account executive"
```

If the abbreviation `CSM` creates noise, require a customer term alongside it:

```text
(CSM AND customer AND (success OR retention OR adoption)) OR "customer success manager"
```

### Implementation and Onboarding Roles

Implementation jobs may be posted under several titles. This string covers the common variants:

```text
("implementation specialist" OR "implementation consultant" OR "onboarding specialist" OR "customer onboarding manager") AND (SaaS OR software OR platform) NOT sales NOT recruiter
```

If you want roles with more project coordination and less help-desk work, add delivery terms:

```text
("implementation manager" OR "onboarding manager" OR "implementation consultant") AND (project OR deployment OR configuration OR training) NOT "technical support" NOT "help desk"
```

For technical implementation roles, include common technical keywords:

```text
("implementation specialist" OR "solutions consultant" OR "technical onboarding") AND (API OR SQL OR integrations OR configuration) AND SaaS NOT developer NOT engineer
```

### Account Manager Roles Adjacent to Customer Success

Account management searches often pull in pure sales roles. Use exclusions to focus on retention and expansion instead of prospecting:

```text
("account manager" OR "client account manager" OR "customer account manager") AND (retention OR renewal OR expansion OR upsell) NOT "account executive" NOT "business development" NOT "cold calling"
```

For post-sales SaaS account management:

```text
("account manager" OR "strategic account manager") AND SaaS AND (renewals OR adoption OR "customer success" OR "net retention") NOT SDR NOT BDR NOT prospecting
```

### Renewals and Customer Retention Roles

Renewals roles can be a good fit if you like customer success but want a more commercial focus:

```text
("renewals manager" OR "renewal specialist" OR "customer retention manager") AND (SaaS OR software OR subscription) NOT collections NOT insurance
```

Add customer health or churn language when you want retention strategy rather than administrative contract work:

```text
("renewals manager" OR "retention manager") AND (churn OR "customer health" OR expansion OR "net revenue retention") NOT collections
```

### Customer Support Roles with Growth Potential

If you are starting in support and want a path toward customer success, avoid generic call-center postings and look for product-facing support roles:

```text
("customer support specialist" OR "technical support specialist" OR "product support specialist") AND (SaaS OR software OR platform) NOT "call center" NOT retail NOT cashier
```

For support roles that include onboarding or success responsibilities:

```text
("customer support" OR "technical support") AND (onboarding OR adoption OR training OR "customer success") AND SaaS NOT "call center"
```

## Remote Customer Success Search Strings

Remote CSM searches should include remote language while filtering out location-only noise:

```text
("customer success manager" OR CSM OR "client success manager") AND (remote OR "work from home" OR distributed) AND (SaaS OR software) NOT "call center" NOT "account executive"
```

For remote implementation roles:

```text
("implementation specialist" OR "onboarding specialist" OR "implementation consultant") AND (remote OR "work from home") AND (SaaS OR software OR integrations) NOT recruiter
```

For Google searches across company career pages, try:

```text
site:greenhouse.io OR site:lever.co "customer success manager" remote SaaS -"account executive"
```

You can also search company career pages directly:

```text
site:jobs.lever.co ("customer success manager" OR CSM) (remote OR "United States") SaaS
```

## Add Industry and Tool Keywords

Customer success roles vary by product type. Add one or two industry or tool terms to sharpen results:

```text
("customer success manager" OR CSM) AND (healthcare OR fintech OR cybersecurity OR edtech) AND SaaS
```

For roles using common customer success platforms:

```text
("customer success manager" OR "customer operations") AND (Gainsight OR ChurnZero OR Totango OR Catalyst)
```

For CRM-heavy roles:

```text
("customer success manager" OR "account manager") AND (Salesforce OR HubSpot) AND (renewals OR adoption OR retention)
```

## Exclusions That Reduce Noise

Common exclusions for customer success searches include:

```text
NOT "call center" NOT cashier NOT retail NOT "account executive" NOT SDR NOT BDR NOT recruiter
```

On Google, use minus signs:

```text
-"call center" -cashier -retail -"account executive" -SDR -BDR -recruiter
```

Be careful not to exclude too much at once. Some CSM postings mention support, sales, or renewals because the role partners with those teams. Start with the worst sources of noise, then tighten the search only if results are still too broad.

## A Simple Customer Success Search Workflow

1. Start with two or three target titles, such as `"customer success manager"`, `"client success manager"`, and `CSM`.
2. Add required context such as `SaaS`, `retention`, `adoption`, `renewals`, or `onboarding`.
3. Exclude obvious mismatches like call-center, retail, and pure sales titles.
4. Run the search on one job board and save the best postings.
5. Copy useful wording from those postings back into your next search string.

For example:

```text
("customer success manager" OR "client success manager" OR CSM) AND (SaaS OR software) AND (retention OR adoption OR renewals) NOT "call center" NOT "account executive"
```

## Final Thoughts

Customer success job titles are flexible, which is useful when you know how to search across them and frustrating when you do not. Boolean search gives you a repeatable way to include the right title variations, require customer-success signals, and remove the roles that do not match your goals.

Start broad, review the first page of results, and then add one filter at a time. A strong customer success search string should help you find better-fit postings without hiding the adjacent roles that could still move your career forward.
